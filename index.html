I'm Mohammed Ali Abobakr (محمد علي أبو بكر), a second-year Computers & Artificial Intelligence student at Sphinx University in Qena, Egypt. My focus is data engineering — designing pipelines and data architectures that stay reliable as they scale.

Alongside that, I work in a fast, iterative development flow, using AI tools to build and refine practical solutions. I integrate large language models into products, from voice assistants to workflow automation, and care about clean architecture as much as the model behind it.

I'm training through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative Data Engineering & AI track, leading the Pharaohs Student Team, and building side projects that push my skills further.
